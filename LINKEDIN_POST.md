PTA popcorn pickup is Thursday at 3:30 PM.

Swimming starts at 4:00 PM.

Each looks manageable in its own app. Together, they create a transportation problem.

That led me to build a personal Family Activity Hub—one parent-controlled view across school communications, activities, tasks, and schedule changes.

Because this involves a child's identity, locations, and daily routine, I started with privacy before intelligence:

- Local processing
- Parent approval
- Minimum data retained
- No child data sent to a third-party AI model

Everything shown in the demo is fictional.

I documented the user scenarios, privacy decisions, architecture, and tradeoffs in this repository.

Parents: how many places do you currently check to manage school and activities?
